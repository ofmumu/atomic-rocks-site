Support

Help with Atomic Reader for iPhone and Mac

Contact

Questions, problems, or ideas: db@atomic.rocks. If something is not working, please say which device and which version of the app you are using (the version is shown when the app starts) and what you expected to happen.

How do I add a feed?

On iPhone, open the drawer (the round button at the bottom left, or swipe in from the left edge) and tap the plus button. On Mac, use the plus button above the sidebar. Enter the address of the feed, or of a site that has one.

How do I mark articles as unread?

On iPhone, swipe an article right to toggle it between read and unread. At the end of a list, swipe the "End of" row to the left to mark the read articles in that list unread, or everything read in that feed, category, or All Articles. In the drawer, touch and hold a feed or category for Mark All as Unread. On Mac, use the right-click menu on a feed, category, or All Articles.

I marked everything as read by mistake.

After marking a list read on iPhone, a message appears at the bottom with an Undo button. Undo restores the articles as unread and takes you back to where you were.

Does my library sync?

Yes, if you are signed into iCloud on your devices. Your feeds, categories, and read and saved articles sync through your own private iCloud account. Text sizes and other display settings stay on each device. If articles or feeds do not appear on another device, check that you are signed into the same iCloud account there and that iCloud is available, then give it a few minutes.

A feed shows a problem.

Feeds that could not be read are marked with a warning, and the list header says how many need attention. Some sites change their feed address or ask you to sign in. Open the feed to see what the app found. If it is a problem with the feed itself, the site's owner would need to fix it.
